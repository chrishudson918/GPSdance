Editor mockups

Three clickable versions of the Editor. There is no real sound, but every button and drag works. All three use a dark look, one row per song, a fixed toolbar, trimming by dragging edges, overlap = crossfade, and zoom-in-and-drag to censor a word. They differ in where the toolbar sits, how tall the rows are, the colors, and where a song's settings (speed, beat, volume) appear.

Option A

  • Toolbar across the top, with a hint for the picked song
  • Medium rows
  • Bright colors
  • Song settings are always visible beside each row: Speed −/+, BPM (click to fix) and Volume −/+
Open option A

Option B

  • Toolbar down the left side
  • Tall rows with a big waveform
  • Soft colors
  • Song settings in a pop-up (the Speed button, the Settings button on the row, or a double-click on the song)
Open option B

Option C

  • Toolbar along the bottom, with bigger buttons
  • Slim rows, so more songs fit without scrolling
  • Neon colors on near-black
  • A settings strip above the toolbar shows the picked song's speed, beat, volume and fades
Open option C

Things to try in each one

  • Click a song, then drag its left or right edge to trim it. Drag the middle to move it.
  • Drag the white dots at a song's top corners to fade in or out.
  • Drag a song so it overlaps the one above: that makes a crossfade. Click the label between them, then Transition, to make it beat-matched or a sound-effect bridge.
  • Click the ruler to move the white line, pick a song, press Split, then Delete a piece.
  • Press Censor, drag across a "word", then Listen and nudge it.
  • Fix a wrong BPM (tap along or type it), try One tempo in the top bar, add a Sound effect and a Section, then Export.
  • Undo anything (Ctrl+Z). Space plays and pauses.
